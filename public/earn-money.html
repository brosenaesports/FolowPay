Earn Money Online by Following, Subscribing and Liking

On FollowPay you collect points by doing small social media tasks and turn them into rewards. It is free to join and you can start as a guest in under a minute. Earning is an extra feature next to the profile booster and SMM panel, and the amounts per task are small, so see it as a bonus rather than a salary.

How to earn

  1. Sign in with Google or continue as a guest.
  2. Open Earn and pick YouTube or Instagram.
  3. Do the task shown: subscribe, follow, like, view, comment or watch.
  4. Tap Verify and the points are added to your balance.

Points per task

TaskPoints
Subscribe (YouTube)+1
Follow (Instagram)+2
Like, view or comment+1 each
Watch time (YouTube)+6 per minute

Complete 3 tasks to unlock 2 skips, so you can pass on tasks you do not want.

Redeem real rewards

  • UPI cash sent to your bank account.
  • Amazon gift cards.
  • Google Play gift cards.

You can also spend points on your own growth with the free profile booster, or invite friends with Refer and earn for extra points.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really earn money on FollowPay?

You earn points that you can redeem for UPI cash or gift cards in the Rewards store. Amounts per task are small, so it works best as a bonus.

Do I need to pay to start earning?

No. Joining and earning are free, and you can continue as a guest.

What can I redeem points for?

UPI cash, Amazon gift cards and Google Play gift cards.

Which tasks give points?

Subscribing, following, liking, viewing, commenting and watching on YouTube and Instagram.
