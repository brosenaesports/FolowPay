Frequently asked questions

What is FollowPay?

FollowPay is a profile booster website with three parts: a free booster where you spend points to grow your profile, a paid SMM panel where you pay in rupees from your own wallet, and an earning side where you collect points and rewards by following, subscribing and liking.

How can I earn money on FollowPay?

Open the Earn page and complete simple tasks such as subscribing to a channel, following a profile, liking, viewing, commenting or watching. Each task gives points. You can redeem points for Amazon gift cards, Google Play gift cards or UPI cash in the Rewards store. Earnings per task are small, so treat it as a bonus, not a guaranteed income.

Is the profile booster really free?

Yes. You earn points for free by doing tasks for other people and spend those points on your own orders. If you do not want to wait, you can also buy points, with packs starting at ₹1 and payment by any UPI app.

Which social media platforms are supported?

The free booster covers YouTube and Instagram. The paid SMM panel lists services for YouTube, Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp Channels, X (Twitter), Facebook, TikTok, Spotify, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Threads and more, depending on the live service catalog.

Do SMM panel orders have a refill?

Services that come with a refill guarantee show a Refill button on your order. It opens 24 hours after the order is completed, and every refill request is tracked in the Refills tab. Services without a refill guarantee do not show the button.

Do I need to link my profile or create an account?

No profile setup is needed. Sign in with Google or continue as a guest, paste the link of your video, reel, post or profile, choose the amount and place the order.
